The book

Città in guerra. Appunti di geopolitica urbana

Francesco Chiodelli, Professor of Urban and Political Geography at the University of Turin (see his website lessisless.it), presents his latest book, Città in guerra. Appunti di geopolitica urbana (Bollati Boringhieri, 2026).

The book explores the urbanisation of contemporary warfare, examining how cities have become targets, instruments, and sites of conflict. Moving between Sarajevo, Gaza, and the increasingly militarised spaces of cities apparently at peace, Francesco invites us to rethink the relationship between urban life, violence, and the possibilities for peace.

The book is in Italian; the event will be in English. It is a good way to be introduced to the book, especially if you are not comfortable reading Italian.
