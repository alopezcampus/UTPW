Programme

Five cycles a year

The Workshop keeps a fixed rhythm. Every cycle lasts two months and takes up a single concept in one session.

2026–2027

Otherwise Urban

Each year has its own emphasis. For 2026–2027 the emphasis is Otherwise Urban. The five cycles of the year take it up one concept at a time.

Format

One concept, one session

Each cycle takes up a single concept and gathers around it in one session. We announce the details of each session as the year goes on: the date, the speakers and the readings. The spirit of the Workshop is set out in the manifesto, which is being rewritten and will be published on the About page.

The year

Cycles

The year opens in January with feminist urban theory and closes in December with a reflection on how we know the city. Open a cycle to read about it; the details of each session will be announced on its page.
