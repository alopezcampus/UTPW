About

A workshop that does not end

The Urban Theory Permanent Workshop is an ongoing, distributed workshop in urban theory. Its participants are doctoral researchers and scholars who work on cities from many places, disciplines and institutions.

The name

Why “permanent”

The project began as the Urban Theory Workshop. A workshop, in the usual sense, is a single event in a single place. This one has no single place and no end date. It continues from cycle to cycle, and the new name says so.

Aims

What the Workshop is for

  • Share knowledge

    Open conversations about urban theory, wherever it is produced.

  • Involve a wide community

    Bring researchers from many places and institutions into the same discussion.

  • Support students and young scholars

    Make room for doctoral researchers and early-career scholars.

Manifesto

We are here to learn how to re-learn

We value knowledge from anywhere. We value knowledge from anyone.

The manifesto is being rewritten for the relaunch. It will be published here, together with the three angles that shape each cycle.
