Urban theory is a collective endeavour that takes shape through the questions we ask, the concepts we use, and the places from which we think.

The URBAN THEORY permanent WORKSHOP aims to provide a permanent space for early-career scholars to interrogate these concepts, bring different perspectives into conversation, and explore other ways of understanding the urban.

As an independent, distributed space for critical urban studies and political ecology, the Workshop connects researchers across institutions and geographies. Through reflections on vocabulary, research discussions, and conversations with authors, we explore what concepts make visible, what they leave out, and how they might be challenged, reworked, and expanded. In doing so, we ask how we might otherwise urban.
