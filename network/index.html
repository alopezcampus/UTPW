Research clusters

Nine research clusters

The Network is one of the cores of this initiative. Its students and researchers are matched to compose our Roundtables. From the members of the Network, we identified nine research clusters. Our long-term goal is to enrich our community by expanding both thematically and geographically.

  • Urban Planning and Governance
  • Urban Ecology
  • Cultural and Gender Studies
  • Housing Studies
  • Social and Environmental Justice
  • Economic Geography
  • Migration and Border Studies
  • Digital Geography
  • Geography of Health

Join

What joining means

  • Stay in the loop

    Receive the newsletter with news of every event the Workshop organises.

  • Present your research

    Be invited to present your own research and debate it with peers whose interests match yours, in the Roundtables format.

  • Act as discussant

    Be asked to discuss other PhD students’ presentations, in the Roundtables format.

  • Share your work

    Share work you have produced during your academic path, if it falls within the remit of the platform. It becomes part of the Contributions.

We are always eager to meet new students and to bring this initiative to those willing to contribute. We hope to see you aboard soon!
