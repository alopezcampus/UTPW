About the collection

About the collection

The contributions stem from master’s courses held in the field of critical urban studies. We reached out to professors at 28 universities across 11 countries. So far, we have collected essays, literature reviews and book reviews from postgraduate students on topics ranging from housing governance and policy to migration, planetary urbanisation, southern urbanism and more.

Members of the Network can propose their own work for this collection.
